# Original media checklist

Before adding a photograph or video to the live Dyaana website, confirm:

- Dyaana created it or holds written commercial usage rights.
- Any recognisable model has signed a commercial model release.
- No supplier, marketplace or competitor image has been copied.
- No visible third-party logo or artwork appears without permission.
- Relove photographs show the actual inspected garment and every disclosed flaw.
- The original high-resolution file and permission record are retained privately.

## Web preparation

- Product photographs: WebP, approximately 1200 × 1600 pixels, ideally below 250 KB.
- Hero photographs: WebP, approximately 1920 × 1080 pixels, ideally below 500 KB.
- Product video: MP4/WebM, short, muted by default and compressed for mobile.
- Add meaningful alternative text for images and captions for videos where needed.
